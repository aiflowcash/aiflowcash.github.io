Privacy Policy – The Creator Cuts Uploader

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This tool is used only by the owner of The Creator Cuts YouTube channel to upload videos to their own channel through the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

With the owner's permission, the tool uses Google OAuth to upload videos and read basic channel and video statistics for the owner's own channel. It does not access any other user's data.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
